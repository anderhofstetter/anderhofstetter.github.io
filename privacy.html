Privacy Policy

App: ClipperTheKingEv3r

Operator: thekingev3r · Contact: anderhofstetter1@gmail.com

Last updated: 17 November 2026

1. Summary

This app is used only by its Operator on the Operator's own TikTok account. It does not collect, sell, or share

personal data of any other person.

2. What the app accesses

  • When the Operator authorizes the app through TikTok Login Kit, the app receives an access token and a refresh

token for the Operator's own account.

  • The app uses the token only to upload the Operator's video files as drafts (scope video.upload).
  • The app does not read the Operator's followers, messages, profile data, or any other user's data.

3. How data is stored

The tokens are stored only on the Operator's own computer, in a local file. They are not sent to any server

operated by the Operator and are not shared with third parties. The Operator can revoke access at any time in

TikTok (Settings > Security > Manage app permissions), and the tokens then stop working.

4. Other services

To prepare videos the Operator uses other tools (for example a messaging app for approving clips and an AI service

to write titles). They receive the Operator's own video clips and captions, not TikTok account data or tokens.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the Operator deletes the local file or revokes access. To request information or deletion,

contact anderhofstetter1@gmail.com.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
